# RFC-0003: Human-in-the-Loop Deliberation

Status: Draft v0.3

## Abstract

This RFC defines the role of humans within OFMM deliberations.

Human participation is considered a fundamental component of collective intelligence and decision quality.

## Motivation

Many AI systems assume a simple interaction model:

Human -> AI -> Answer

Enterprise decision making rarely works this way.

Complex decisions require review, correction, interpretation and accountability.

## Human-in-the-Loop Principle

OFMM assumes humans participate throughout deliberation rather than only at the beginning or end.

## Human Responsibilities

- Define objectives
- Introduce context
- Challenge assumptions
- Review proposals
- Validate conclusions
- Approve decisions

## Guidance Model

Human Mind Members MAY:

- redirect deliberations
- invite experts
- request additional evidence
- reject insufficient reasoning

## Consequential Decisions

For decisions involving security, legal, financial, privacy, compliance or strategic impact, at least one Human Mind Member SHOULD participate.

## Benefits

- accountability
- reduced hallucination impact
- better risk identification
- organizational trust

## Mission Statement

Humans remain active participants in reasoning, not passive consumers of AI output.
