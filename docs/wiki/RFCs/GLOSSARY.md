# OFMM Glossary

## Mind Member
Participant capable of contributing reasoning, expertise, analysis or decisions.

## Human Mind Member
Participant whose reasoning is performed by one or more humans.

## Artificial Mind Member
Participant whose reasoning is performed entirely by AI systems.

## Hybrid Mind Member
Participant whose reasoning emerges from collaboration between humans and AI.

## Deliberation
Structured discussion among Mind Members.

## Proposal
Suggested solution, recommendation or position.

## Challenge
Constructive criticism intended to improve reasoning quality.

## Consensus
Sufficient agreement to proceed.

## Decision
Accepted outcome of a deliberation.

## Bottleneck
Participant, dependency or process preventing progress.
