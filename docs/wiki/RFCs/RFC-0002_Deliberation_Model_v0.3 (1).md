# RFC-0002: Deliberation Model

**Status:** Draft

**Version:** 0.3

---

# Abstract

This document defines the deliberation model used by OFMM.

A deliberation is the central activity of OFMM and represents a structured process through which Mind Members collaboratively explore problems and make decisions.

---

# 1. Purpose

Deliberation exists to support understanding, analysis, planning, evaluation, consensus formation, and decision making.

The objective is not speed.

The objective is decision quality.

---

# 2. Deliberation Lifecycle

```text
Question
   ↓
Proposal
   ↓
Analysis
   ↓
Challenge
   ↓
Refinement
   ↓
Consensus
   ↓
Decision
```

---

# 3. Question

Every deliberation begins with a clearly defined question.

A deliberation MUST define its objective.

---

# 4. Proposal

A Proposal represents a potential answer or course of action.

A Proposal SHOULD contain rationale, assumptions, author, and timestamp.

---

# 5. Analysis

Mind Members evaluate proposals using expertise, evidence, experience, and reasoning.

---

# 6. Challenge

Challenge is a mandatory reasoning mechanism.

Challenge is considered collaboration.

---

# 7. Refinement

Proposals evolve through continuous refinement.

---

# 8. Human-Guided Deliberation

OFMM does not assume autonomous end-to-end reasoning.

Human participants are expected to redirect discussions, request clarification, challenge conclusions, and validate outcomes.

---

# 9. Consensus

Consensus exists when significant objections have been reviewed and addressed.

---

# 10. Decision

A decision SHOULD contain rationale, supporting proposals, unresolved concerns, and responsible owner.

---

# 11. Deliberation Modes

- Collaborative Mode
- Debate Mode
- Challenger Mode
- Expert Panel Mode
- Consensus Mode

---

# 12. Dynamic Participation

Mind Members may be invited during an active deliberation.

---

# 13. Asynchronous Deliberation

Participation does not need to be immediate.

---

# 14. Transparency

Participants SHOULD be able to determine current state, active participants, waiting dependencies, bottlenecks, and pending decisions.

---

# 15. Bottlenecks

A deliberation bottleneck is any dependency preventing progress.

Bottlenecks SHOULD be visible.

---

# Mission Statement

> The purpose of OFMM is not merely to connect Mind Members.
>
> The purpose of OFMM is to enable Mind Members to think together.
