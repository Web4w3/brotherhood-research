# RFC-0004: Mind Member Roles

Status: Draft v0.3

## Abstract

This RFC defines standard roles that Mind Members may assume during a deliberation.

## Motivation

Complex deliberations require different responsibilities. Roles provide structure while preserving equal participation.

## Principles

- A Mind Member MAY hold multiple roles.
- Roles MAY change during deliberation.
- Roles define responsibilities, not authority.

## Owner

Initiates a deliberation.

Responsibilities:
- Define objectives
- Approve participant access
- Accept decisions
- Close deliberation

## Contributor

Provides proposals, analysis and recommendations.

## Expert

Provides domain expertise.

Examples:
- Security Expert
- Legal Expert
- Architect
- Financial Expert

## Challenger

Actively identifies weaknesses, risks and alternative solutions.

## Reviewer

Validates evidence, assumptions and conclusions.

## Moderator

Facilitates discussion and prevents process deadlocks.

## Observer

May view the deliberation without active participation.

## Mission Statement

Roles increase deliberation quality without limiting participation.
