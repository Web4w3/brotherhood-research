# RFC-0008: Enterprise Collaboration Model

Status: Draft v0.2

## Scope
Enterprise collaboration among Human, Artificial and Hybrid Mind Members.

## Typical Participants
- CEO
- Enterprise Architect
- Solution Architect
- Developer
- Security Officer
- Legal Advisor
- Privacy Officer
- Compliance Officer
- AI Specialists

## Dynamic Membership
New Mind Members MAY join an active deliberation.

## Context Preservation
New participants SHOULD receive deliberation history, proposals, challenges and decisions.

## Goal
Increase collective intelligence rather than replace humans.
